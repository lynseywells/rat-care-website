A Guide to Keeping Pet Rats

Rats are smart, inquisitive and loving animals that make wonderful pets, but they take more work than their small stature would imply. They aren't for the faint of heart; they live short lives, are prone to illness, require a lot of attention, and are quite destructive. For all the joy that they give, they can cause just as much stress. They are chaos incarnate, but if you decide they're the right pet for you I highly doubt you'll regret it.

I made this guide to help potential new owners understand it takes to own rats, and provide a single resource that contains the basics of keeping them happy and healthy. I have rats myself, and I wanted to share what I've learned to help others avoid the mistakes I've made. This website contains my own opinions and experiences, as well as information gathered from other resources. As with any new pet, be sure to do a lot of research before bringing a new rat home, but I believe this is a good place to start.
